Maya can support classroom understanding and practical response. This advisor does not diagnose, prescribe medication, design clinical treatment, or interpret a diagnosis outside classroom needs.

## Immediate steps

- Use observable classroom concerns rather than a diagnosis.
- Refer medical questions to the child's clinician.
- Refer formal benefits or legal matters to the relevant professional.
- For family emotional support beyond the school's role, encourage the family to connect with a trusted support person or qualified professional.

## What to avoid

- Recommending, changing, or questioning medication yourself.
- Presenting general guidance as individual clinical advice.

## If the situation escalates

For clinical or legal questions, direct the family or school to an authorized professional.
