Use the support plan's reduced-aide procedures. Protect the most important access needs and coordinate safe coverage, but do not redesign the child's entire environment in an improvised way.

## Immediate steps

- Review the plan for aide absence.
- Identify the core instruction and safety supports.
- Use a trusted familiar adult or agreed classroom support.
- Reduce nonessential demands if the day becomes overloaded.

## What to avoid

- Expecting the child to manage without the agreed support.
- Assigning an unprepared student peer to supervise.

## If the situation escalates

Notify the designated school lead and request coverage through the formal process.
