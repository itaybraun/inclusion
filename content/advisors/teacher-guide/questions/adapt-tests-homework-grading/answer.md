Adapt access and presentation when allowed, while preserving the learning goal. Accommodation changes how work is demonstrated or completed; it should not silently lower standards or give an unfair advantage.

## Immediate steps

- Consult the approved learning or assessment plan.
- Clarify whether extra time, a quiet room, a scribe, or alternative format is appropriate.
- Explain grading rules consistently.
- Use the child's strengths to assess understanding.

## What to avoid

- Making informal accommodations that are not recorded or consistent.
- Penalizing motor, sensory, or communication differences as lack of knowledge.

## If the situation escalates

Use the school counselor or inclusion lead to confirm formal assessment accommodations.
