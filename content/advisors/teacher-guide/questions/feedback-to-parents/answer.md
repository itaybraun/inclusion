Lead with concrete strengths and examples, then describe the concern and its impact. Propose one or two practical next steps. Ask the parent what context or strategies could help.

## Immediate steps

- Prepare two observations and one support suggestion.
- Use specific behavior rather than character judgments.
- Separate a parent concern from a diagnosis.
- Agree on a follow-up date.

## What to avoid

- Starting with every problem.
- Using alarming certainty or hidden blame.
- Suggesting medical or therapeutic conclusions.

## If the situation escalates

Ask a counselor or principal to join if the meeting becomes unsafe or highly disputed.
