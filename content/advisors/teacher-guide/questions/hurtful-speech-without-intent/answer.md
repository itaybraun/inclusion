Respond to both intent and impact. The student may not have understood the hurtful effect, but the behavior still affects others. Explain in concrete, private language and provide a repair action.

## Immediate steps

- Stop the behavior calmly.
- Name the observable impact without humiliation.
- Explain the expected replacement behavior.
- Support a short, sincere repair if appropriate.

## What to avoid

- Assuming no intent means no accountability.
- Giving a public lecture or demanding immediate emotional insight.

## If the situation escalates

Use the support team if the behavior is frequent or indicates distress or harm.
