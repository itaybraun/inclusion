Do not introduce or explain the diagnosis unless the parent or guardian has authorized you and the child is ready for that conversation. Use concrete, respectful language about strengths and support needs. Coordinate with the family and school counselor on who will tell the child and when.

## Immediate steps

- Confirm the family's wishes with the parent or guardian.
- Avoid labels or explanations that imply something is wrong with the child.
- Follow the agreed support plan.
- Let the family lead direct diagnostic disclosure.

## What to avoid

- Surprising the child with the word or label.
- Presenting a diagnosis as blame, limitation, or permanent prediction.

## If the situation escalates

Ask the school counselor or the family's approved professional to guide a difficult disclosure.
