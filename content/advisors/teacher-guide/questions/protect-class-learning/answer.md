Maintain clear whole-class boundaries while making an individual plan for access. A safety or learning interruption can be addressed privately and promptly without removing the student or defining them by the incident.

## Immediate steps

- State a neutral group rule.
- Use the student's known break or support route.
- Continue the class with a minimal disruption.
- Meet afterward to teach replacement behavior.

## What to avoid

- Publicly punishing the whole class because of one student's needs.
- Using exclusion as the default response.

## If the situation escalates

Coordinate with the school team if the plan repeatedly fails or safety is at risk.
