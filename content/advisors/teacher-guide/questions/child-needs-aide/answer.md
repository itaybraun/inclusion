Document specific needs and the impact on access, learning, and safety. Request a formal school review rather than promising or denying an aide yourself.

## Immediate steps

- Keep factual observations across settings and times.
- Use the existing support plan to identify gaps.
- Request a meeting with the inclusion lead or counselor.
- Ask what interim supports can begin now.

## What to avoid

- Basing a request only on inconvenience or diagnosis.
- Making the request in front of the child or class.

## If the situation escalates

Follow your jurisdiction's formal evaluation or special-support process.
