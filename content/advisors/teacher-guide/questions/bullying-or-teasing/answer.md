Treat bullying as a behavioral and safeguarding concern, not a conflict to be solved by asking the two children to be friends. Address the incident promptly, protect the targeted child, and follow a consistent process for the person who bullied.

## Immediate steps

- Separate the student from the harm or audience.
- Listen to the student without forcing a retelling.
- Record the facts and witnesses.
- Apply the school's anti-bullying process.
- Check for repeated or digital incidents.

## What to avoid

- Blaming the autistic child for 'being difficult.'
- Using the incident to disclose the child's diagnosis.
- Telling the child to ignore repeated harm.

## If the situation escalates

Notify the principal or designated safeguarding lead according to school policy.
