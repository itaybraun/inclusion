Escalate when there is immediate danger, repeated safeguarding concerns, significant learning or health changes, unresolved bullying, or a need for formal specialist assessment or resources.

## Immediate steps

- Use emergency procedures for immediate danger.
- Record facts promptly.
- Notify the designated counselor or principal under policy.
- Keep the parent informed according to the school's process.

## What to avoid

- Delaying urgent safety concerns to wait for a diagnosis.
- Expecting a classroom teacher to manage all clinical or legal issues.

## If the situation escalates

The outside professional should be selected through the family or authorized school process.
