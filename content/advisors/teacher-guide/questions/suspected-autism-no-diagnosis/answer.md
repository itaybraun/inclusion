You may describe observable concerns and refer the family to appropriate school or health professionals. Do not tell the family that they have a diagnosis or withhold normal supports based on missing paperwork unless policy requires it.

## Immediate steps

- Record observable patterns across time and settings.
- Meet with the family and listen to their perspective.
- Offer reasonable classroom supports within existing policy.
- Use the school's referral process.

## What to avoid

- Using diagnostic labels informally.
- Waiting for diagnosis before protecting access or safety.

## If the situation escalates

Refer to the school counselor and the family's health or diagnostic professional.
