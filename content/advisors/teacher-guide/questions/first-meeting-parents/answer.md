Begin by asking what helps the child feel understood and successful. Keep the conversation collaborative and avoid treating the parent as a source of a problem to solve. Agree on the best communication channel and a small set of support priorities.

## Immediate steps

- Ask about communication and sensory preferences.
- Ask which routines reduce stress and which situations are difficult.
- Ask what strengths the child wants recognized.
- Ask how and when the family wants school updates.

## What to avoid

- Using a deficit-only checklist.
- Asking the parent to prove a diagnosis at the first meeting.
- Making promises outside the school's authority.

## If the situation escalates

Invite a counselor or inclusion professional when the discussion requires formal planning.
