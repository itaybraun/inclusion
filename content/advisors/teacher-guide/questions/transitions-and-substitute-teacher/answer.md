Use predictability. A student is more likely to manage a change when they know what is happening, what remains the same, and what is different. Provide the change in the child's preferred format.

## Immediate steps

- Give advance notice with simple, visual language.
- Mark the order of activities or the change in the schedule.
- Prepare a familiar support or a familiar adult.
- Brief the substitute on one-page practical needs.

## What to avoid

- Hiding a change until the last moment as a test.
- Giving too much detail in a stressful moment.
- Removing a support because 'the student should cope.'

## If the situation escalates

Use formal support planning if transitions repeatedly cause significant distress or unsafe behavior.
