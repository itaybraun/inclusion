There is no single checklist that defines every autistic student. Ten useful starting points are: the child is an individual; listen to behavior as communication; preserve autonomy and dignity; sensory input can affect learning; predictability helps; one demand can have a cumulative effect; communication may be verbal or non-verbal; strengths are assets; distress is not bad behavior; and the family and child are partners.

## Immediate steps

- Get the child's approved support plan.
- Ask what helps before assuming.
- Observe patterns, not isolated moments.
- Build a few reliable supports into daily routines.

## What to avoid

- Assuming all autistic students share the same traits.
- Treating eye contact, silence, or compliance as the main indicators of wellbeing.

## If the situation escalates

Use your school's support team when you need individualized professional guidance.
