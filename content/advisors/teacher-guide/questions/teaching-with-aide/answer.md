An aide should increase access and participation, not become the only person who communicates or expects work. Coordinate a role that supports gradually increasing independence.

## Immediate steps

- Agree on who leads instruction, support, and safety response.
- Give the aide the key lesson goal and prompts.
- Use prompts that move from direct support toward independence.
- Share observations rather than making judgments.

## What to avoid

- Keeping the aide standing beside the child throughout all activities.
- Allowing the aide to correct the whole class or replace the teacher.

## If the situation escalates

Bring unresolved role or safeguarding questions to the principal or support coordinator.
