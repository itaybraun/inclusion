Do not announce a diagnosis to the whole class on your own. Start with the parent or guardian and follow the child's age, wishes, and school policy. If disclosure is approved, explain only what the class needs to understand and support, such as 'this student may need quiet or extra processing time,' not a private medical history.

## Immediate steps

- Ask the parent or guardian whether and how the child has been told.
- Use the child's support plan and school disclosure policy.
- If approved, give peers simple facts and consistent classroom expectations.
- Respect the child's privacy at all times.

## What to avoid

- Announcing a diagnosis for 'justification' or convenience.
- Sharing information the student has not been told or has asked to keep private.
- Making peers responsible for the child's support.

## If the situation escalates

Escalate conflicts or safeguarding concerns to the school's designated professional.
