Plan for sensory demands before the event. Give the child information about noise, length, waiting, people, and possible exits. Offer a predictable way to reduce or pause sensory input.

## Immediate steps

- Review the schedule and location in advance.
- Offer headphones, a quiet place, or a shorter route if included in the plan.
- Assign a clear support adult.
- Create an agreed exit or rest signal.

## What to avoid

- Requiring full participation in every sensory setting.
- Using surprise, public pressure, or removal as punishment.

## If the situation escalates

Work with the family and school team if the setting creates repeated severe distress.
