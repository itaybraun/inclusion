Answer with inclusive, need-based language. It is usually enough to say that every student receives support matched to their needs and that personal information is private. Do not disclose the diagnosis or invite peers to compare experiences.

## Immediate steps

- Acknowledge the question fairly.
- Explain the visible support, not the private diagnosis.
- Reinforce equal dignity and school rules.
- Offer to discuss the parent concern through the agreed school channel.

## What to avoid

- Saying the child is 'special' in a way that singles them out.
- Comparing students or revealing accommodations.

## If the situation escalates

Refer persistent or discriminatory concerns to the principal or school inclusion lead.
