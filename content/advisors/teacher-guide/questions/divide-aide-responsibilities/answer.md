The teacher owns curriculum, classroom expectations, and whole-class instruction. The aide supports the student's access to those goals and should follow agreed prompts and boundaries.

## Immediate steps

- Create a simple responsibility chart.
- Agree who gives instructions, prompts, breaks, and reports.
- Set a plan for peer interaction and independence.
- Review what happened at the end of the day.

## What to avoid

- Leaving the aide to decide educational goals alone.
- Having the teacher and aide give conflicting instructions.

## If the situation escalates

Ask the school inclusion lead to mediate if responsibilities remain unclear.
