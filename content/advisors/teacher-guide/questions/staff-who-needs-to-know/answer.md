Share with staff who need the information to supervise, teach, support, or protect the student. The usual rule is the minimum necessary detail. The teaching need should determine what is shared, not general staff curiosity.

## Immediate steps

- Use the formal support plan or need-to-know summary.
- Tell staff about communication, sensory, and safety supports.
- Explain the agreed confidentiality boundaries.
- Update the information as routines or needs change.

## What to avoid

- Forwarding diagnostic reports to people who do not have a professional need.
- Discussing the child's case casually in staff areas.

## If the situation escalates

When in doubt, ask the principal or designated privacy lead rather than deciding alone.
