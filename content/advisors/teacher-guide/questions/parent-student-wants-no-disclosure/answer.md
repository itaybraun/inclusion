Keep the information confidential within the limits of professional need and applicable school policy. Share only the functional information staff need to provide safe support. Clarify that 'not telling' does not mean ignoring bullying, serious needs, or urgent safety concerns.

## Immediate steps

- Thank the parent or student for being clear.
- Ask what information they are comfortable sharing and with whom.
- Create a support plan based on needs rather than diagnostic labels.
- Document the agreed scope of disclosure.

## What to avoid

- Pressuring the family or child to disclose.
- Telling peers or staff more than was agreed.
- Promising absolute secrecy if safeguarding or safety requires information to be shared.

## If the situation escalates

If the disagreement affects safety or equal access, involve the designated school lead under local policy.
