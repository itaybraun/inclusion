A crisis can include immediate risk of injury, inability to remain safe, severe panic, self-harm statements, or behavior that cannot be contained by ordinary supports. Follow the school's written emergency plan and local emergency guidance.

## Immediate steps

- Move other people away from danger if it is safe to do so.
- Contact the designated crisis lead and emergency services when indicated.
- Use only approved de-escalation or safety procedures.
- Keep communication calm and brief.
- Document the sequence of events after the situation.

## What to avoid

- Restrictive or punitive actions not approved by the school.
- Crowding, arguing, or announcing the crisis to the class.

## If the situation escalates

Call local emergency services for imminent danger or follow your country's emergency number.
