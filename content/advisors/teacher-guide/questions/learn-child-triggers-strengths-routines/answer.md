Learn from several sources: the parent or guardian, the child, the aide, and careful classroom observation. Ask for examples rather than general labels. Keep a short log of antecedents, observable behavior, effective supports, and the time needed to recover.

## Immediate steps

- Meet with the family and child if appropriate.
- Ask what is easy and enjoyable for the child.
- Record patterns across several days.
- Test one small support and check whether it helps.

## What to avoid

- Writing a diagnosis from a behavior log.
- Collecting unnecessary personal information.
- Treating a trigger that appears in one context as universal.

## If the situation escalates

Bring repeated or serious patterns to the school counselor or qualified support team.
