Agree on a predictable routine rather than sending updates only during problems. Use brief, two-way communication and prioritize time-sensitive concerns.

## Immediate steps

- Ask the family which channel and language they prefer.
- Use a short weekly or school-approved format.
- Include one success or strength.
- Describe one concern and the next step.

## What to avoid

- Lengthy incident logs sent by surprise.
- Using multiple staff contacts without coordination.

## If the situation escalates

Use a scheduled meeting for complex or disputed matters.
