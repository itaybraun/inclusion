Create structured, optional opportunities for connection. Friendship grows through shared interests, predictable routines, and supported interaction rather than forced pairing.

## Immediate steps

- Ask the child which peers or activities they prefer.
- Set up small, purposeful group activities.
- Coach communication privately or through role-play.
- Allow the child to opt out of social moments.

## What to avoid

- Assigning a peer to be a permanent helper or friend.
- Using public reward for social compliance.

## If the situation escalates

Seek support from the school counselor if loneliness or bullying persists.
