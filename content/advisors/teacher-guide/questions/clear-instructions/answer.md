Give one clear, concrete instruction at a time. Use short language and show or write the result you expect. Allow extra processing time and check understanding by asking the child to explain or demonstrate, not just say yes.

## Immediate steps

- Remove irrelevant words and yes/no ambiguity.
- Pair speech with a visual or written cue if useful.
- Give the instruction from a calm distance.
- Check understanding privately.

## What to avoid

- Using sarcasm, rapid questions, or abstract idioms without explanation.
- Repeating loudly or stacking several instructions.

## If the situation escalates

Ask the school's communication or inclusion specialist for support with ongoing comprehension barriers.
