First determine whether refusal is communication, overwhelm, exhaustion, pain, fear, or a mismatch in the task. Reduce pressure and establish a safe way to pause. Do not assume that leaving is defiance.

## Immediate steps

- Check immediate safety.
- Use a brief, neutral instruction and offer two safe options.
- Look at the pace, clarity, sensory conditions, and task demand.
- Agree later on an appropriate re-entry or catch-up plan.

## What to avoid

- Blocking the child from a safe exit.
- Public argument or threats.
- Using forced compliance as the default.

## If the situation escalates

Follow the school absence or safeguarding process if leaving creates danger or regular disruption.
