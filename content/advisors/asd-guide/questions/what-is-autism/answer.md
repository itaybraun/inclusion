Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), often called autism, is a neurodevelopmental condition. It describes a pattern of differences in social communication and interaction, together with restricted or repetitive behaviors, interests, or activities. Sensory experiences may also be different.

Autism begins during development, although it may be recognized or diagnosed at different ages. It is not caused by parenting, and it is not an illness that someone can catch. Autistic people vary widely: each person has an individual profile of communication, interests, strengths, challenges, and support needs.

An autism diagnosis is made by qualified professionals using developmental history and a broader assessment. A single behavior or trait is not enough to determine whether someone is autistic.
