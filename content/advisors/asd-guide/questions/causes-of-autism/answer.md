There is no single known cause of autism. Research indicates that genetics play an important role, and many different genes may be involved. Other developmental factors may also contribute, but the science is complex and does not explain every person's autism.

Autism is not caused by parenting or by a lack of affection. Extensive research has found no evidence that vaccines cause autism.

Knowing possible contributing factors cannot predict an individual person's abilities, support needs, or future. For questions about a specific child's development, a qualified health professional can discuss assessment and family concerns without assuming a cause.
